Harbor Travel Platform

Client
Harbor Collective
Category
Web
Year
2026
Services
UX, Front-end, DAM

Overview

Harbor needed a public booking experience that stayed in lockstep with a growing media library. Nuvora became the source of truth for photography, rates sheets, and destination films.

Challenge

Seasonal campaigns used outdated stills because the website CMS and the studio drive never agreed on a latest version.

Solution

We mapped collections to destination pages, added review gates, and published only signed-off assets through a read-only API.
