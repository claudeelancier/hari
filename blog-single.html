How studios keep reviews inside the library

By Priya Raman · Apr 12, 2026 · Workflow

Most review chaos is not a taste problem. It is a location problem. Comments live in chat, stills live on a drive, and the version that got approved is never the one that shipped.

Put the conversation on the file

When a producer can draw on a frame and assign a reviewer, the thread cannot drift. Nuvora stores those notes with the version, not as a screenshot in someone's camera roll.

“If the comment cannot find the pixel, it is not a comment. It is folklore.”

A Friday ritual

  • Close or snooze every open review older than seven days.
  • Promote one version to master.
  • Archive the rest instead of deleting on sight.

Do this for a month and the “which file?” messages taper off. People trust the library because it is current.

What we will not automate

Taste still belongs to humans. Automation can gather files and ping owners. It should not pretend a like count is a creative decision.
